# WF-monthly-review: Monthly Operating Review

**Executed by:** Ops-Agent

---

## Frequency

Last Friday of the month, prepare the context 2 days in advance.

## Preparation steps (2 days before)

1. **Read** the last 4 weeks of `Strategy/DRI-Roadmap-2026/DRI-Evidence/`
2. **Read** `Strategy/DRI-Roadmap-2026/DRI-Map.md`: current status
3. **Identify** the 3–5 most important topics for the agenda:
   - Systemic blockers that were not resolved in the cycle
   - OKRs at risk of not being met before the deadline
   - Business decisions that require [[CEO]]
   - Process improvements identified in the cycles
4. **Generate** a draft agenda in `Operations/Cadences/Monthly-Review/YYYY-MM.md`
5. **Present** to [[Chief of Staff]] for adjustments

## During the meeting

Meeting-Agent processes the transcript → minutes in `Meetings/Operating-Reviews/YYYY-MM.md`

## Post-meeting

Ops-Agent:
1. Updates DRI Map with the changes decided
2. Records strategic decisions in `Strategy/Decisions/`
3. Proposes updates to SOPs or policies if process changes arose
