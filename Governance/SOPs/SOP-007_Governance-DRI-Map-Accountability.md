# SOP-007: DRI Map Governance and Accountability

> **Draft for Rodney's review.** Born from strategic alignment with the team (2026-07-01). Formalizes how the execution of the [[DRI-Map]] is governed and how things are ensured to happen.

## Purpose

Define how FinCo governs the execution of its strategic portfolio (the DRI Map and its epics) and how accountability is rendered. This is not operational monitoring (that is SOP-003); it is the governance of **strategic progress**: who is responsible for what, at what cadence it is reviewed, and how execution is ensured.

## Scope

Applies to all DRIs of DRI Map components and of the current quarter's epics. The Chief of Staff facilitates; each DRI executes and is accountable.

## Fundamental rule

**Each DRI is the owner of their component/epic execution plan.** Task breakdown, instrumentation, and follow-up are entirely the DRI's responsibility, **there is no Project Manager who plans for them, nor does the Chief of Staff do anyone's work**. Each epic is instrumented as a project in Linear, and the DRI links their issues to that epic.

> Culture: at FinCo, the truly responsible people are the individuals, not the tools or agents. "It was Atlas' fault" is not valid. We seek a healthy accountability culture, different from [[GroupCo]], where the PM cooked the plan and handed it over. Here, the axis owner decides and executes.

## Governance cadence

| Horizon | Forum | Frequency | What is reviewed |
|---|---|---|---|
| Day-to-day | Agentic standup + Slack | Daily | Immediate blockers (immediate correction) |
| Week | Weekly cycle + Team Sync (SOP-001) | Weekly | Tactical/minor items: cycle issues, demos |
| **Fortnight** | **Strategic Progress Review** | **Biweekly** | **Each epic DRI presents results and their evidence pack** |
| Quarter | Roadmap adjustment with Alex Carver | Per quarter | Strategic direction for the following quarter |

The **Strategic Progress Review** is the heart of this SOP: it is biweekly (not weekly) because on certain topics not enough progress is made in a week, and the minor items are already covered in the cycle and team syncs.

## Steps

### 1: Evidence pack per DRI
Each DRI maintains an evidence package (weekly or biweekly) of their component/epic, following [[SOP-004_DRI-Weekly-Evidence|SOP-004]]. The evidence may reference Linear issues, minutes, and deliverables.

### 2: Strategic Progress Review (biweekly)
In a biweekly session, each quarter epic DRI **presents** their progress vs. commitments, their evidence pack, and their blockers. Reviewed against the DRI Map and the [[OKRs]].

### 3: Execution in Linear
Each epic is a project in Linear; the DRI does their breakdown and links their issues. The live state of work lives in Linear; the durable context lives in the vault.

### 4: Escalation
Blockers unresolved >7 days are escalated (see component 14 of the DRI Map). A DRI who does not present evidence for two consecutive fortnights is spoken to directly.

## Quality metrics

- 100% of epic DRIs present evidence at each biweekly review.
- Each epic has its project in Linear with linked issues.
- 0 "inherited" task plans, each DRI made their own.
- The accountability culture is perceived: DRIs bring solutions and commitments, not excuses.

## Executed by

Facilitated by the [[Chief of Staff]]. Supported by the [[Ops-Agent]] (consolidates evidence) and the [[OKR-Agent]] (proposes OKR updates under Gate). See [[SOP-004_DRI-Weekly-Evidence]] (evidence) and [[SOP-001_Weekly-Cycle]] (weekly cadence).
