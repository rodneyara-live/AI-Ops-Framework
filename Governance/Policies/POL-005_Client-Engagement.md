# POL-005: Client Engagement Policy

> **Status: DRAFT.** To be developed alongside the Go-to-Market Lead when the commercial pipeline is more structured.

---

## Base principle

FinCo does not sell human attention; it sells agentic results. Every client interaction must reinforce that the value lies in the system, not in the account manager.

---

## Accepted client profile

A client is suitable for FinCo if:

- They have a 0–90 day delinquency portfolio.
- Their debtors are in the Dominican Republic.
- They can operate the Creditor Portal autonomously.
- They do not require an assigned human supervisor or weekly portfolio review meetings.
- They accept a per-management pricing model (not per-recovery).

A client is **not suitable** if they require intensive human attention, have predominantly >90-day delinquency, or need active judicial collection.

---

## Service commitments

| Element | Commitment |
|---|---|
| Onboarding | The client uploads their portfolio to the Creditor Portal. Process documented in `Governance/SOPs/SOP-007_Client-Onboarding.md` (to be created). |
| Communication with debtors | Only through Nova, voice, and SMS within POL-003 parameters. |
| Reports | Activity dashboard available in the Creditor Portal. |
| Support | `#finco-support` Slack channel (or equivalent) + agreed response time. |

---

## Client gates

Before activating any mass dispatch or campaign for a new client:

1. Operations Manager verifies the client complies with POL-003.
2. CEO FinCo approves the first dispatch (client dispatch gate).
3. The first dispatch always has a reduced test batch before full dispatch.
