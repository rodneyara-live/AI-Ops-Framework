# POL-004: Talent Acquisition Policy

> **Status: DRAFT.** This policy establishes hiring principles. The agentic screening process will be designed when the first external role search is activated.

---

## Base principle

FinCo only hires when the new person owns an enduring loop that cannot be handled by the current team plus the agents.

> "Hiring is not growing; it's committing. Every hire must be able to demonstrate that their work cannot be replaced by a well-designed process plus a well-trained agent."

---

## Bar for entry

Every candidate must be able to:

- Write clear specs or operational artifacts.
- Use AI agents directly in their work.
- Maintain their part of the vault (or its equivalent in their domain).
- Own a client or system metric.
- Show up to meetings with prototypes, dashboards, or decisions, not slides.

---

## Projected hiring sequence (pre-seed)

| Timing | Role | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Month 3–4 | Founding CTO | Architecture, security, engineering system. First use of funds. |
| Month 4 | Head of Ops/Success | Client results, onboarding, legal processes. |
| Month 4–5 | Mid/Senior Engineer | Reduce single-person technical risk. |
| Month 6 | PM | Reduce [[CEO]] cognitive load, convert client learnings into specs. |
| Month 9–12 | Head of Sales/GTM | Convert pilots into scale. |

**Explicitly out of pre-seed scope:** traditional PM, standalone Data Engineer, CMO, full-time CFO, VP layers.

---

## Agentic process (to be defined)

When a role search is activated, the Talent-Agent will execute the first screening:

- Profile review against the defined entry bar.
- Generation of role-specific evaluation questions.
- Synthesis of candidate signals for human review.
- The human always makes the final advancement decision.

The detailed process design will be documented in `Governance/SOPs/SOP-006_Talent-Acquisition.md` when the first search is activated.
