# Director Comercial

> Occupant: [[Tomas-Navarro]]. Role formalized 2026-07-03 (vault audit, finding A5), Team.md erroneously marked him as "Guest (inactive)" when in fact he is active and DRI of component 13 (Go-to-Market) of the [[DRI-Map]] and of epic Q3-E5 ([[Roadmap-2026]]).

## Role purpose

Lead the commercial function of [[FinCo]]: pricing model, commercial proposition, prospect pipeline, and the inbound lead channel (web). This role converts prospect interest into an active account managed by FinCo.

---

## Responsibilities

**Commercial model**
- Define and maintain FinCo's pricing model.
- Build and maintain the sales kit (presentation, proposal, autonomous demo) together with Marketing.

**Pipeline and prospecting**
- Be DRI of accounts in the active pipeline (e.g., Quicklend), owner of the proposal and follow-up until close or discard.
- Follow up on the inbound channel: lead forms on finco.io and groupco.io, qualification and routing to MDA or automated email.

**Reporting**
- Keep component 13 (Go-to-Market) of the DRI Map updated: status, progress, and next action.
- Report the status of commercial KRs from the OKR scorecard.

---

## Role competencies

**Technical**
- Pricing models for B2B/fintech services.
- CRM and pipeline management (Odoo).

**Operational**
- Clear commercial communication with prospects and creditors.
- Follow-up discipline; nothing is lost in the pipeline without a documented reason.

---

## Scope of authority

| Decision | Authority |
|---|---|
| Minor pricing model adjustments within approved ranges | Autonomous |
| Structural changes to the pricing model | Gate: CEO FinCo |
| Commercial commitments with a prospect (terms, out-of-range discounts) | Gate: CEO FinCo |
