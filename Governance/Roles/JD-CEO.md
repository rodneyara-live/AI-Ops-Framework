# CEO / Chief AI Officer

## Role purpose

The CEO of FinCo is the founder and chief decision-maker of the company. Their role is to define the strategic vision, design product and technology architecture, and ensure FinCo fulfills its mission of transforming collections in Latin America through artificial intelligence. As Chief AI Officer, they are the final authority on AI decisions shaping the product and the company's agentic operating system.

This role reports to the executive sponsor of Grupo GroupCo and is the highest internal authority level at FinCo. Their time is optimized for vision, architecture, and strategic relationships, not day-to-day operational management.

---

## Responsibilities

**Vision and strategy**
- Define FinCo's strategic direction: what we build, for whom, and why
- Make roadmap decisions with medium- and long-term business impact
- Represent FinCo before strategic clients, investors, and Grupo GroupCo
- Establish the company's values, culture, and operating standards

**Product and AI architecture**
- Design the architecture of AI agents (Conductor, Nova, Strategy Engine) and their interaction flows
- Approve technical decisions impacting client or creditor experience
- Define the AI-first principles guiding product development
- Oversee implementation of AI capabilities in FinCo's products

**Gates and final authority**
- Approve external commitments: contracts, partnerships, client agreements
- Approve production changes impacting debtor or creditor operations
- Approve roadmap decisions modifying active cycle scope
- Approve team additions and organizational structure changes

**Relationship with the team**
- Ensure the Chief of Staff has the context and authority to operate the company's operating system
- Participate in the monthly Operating Review as reviewer and high-level decision-maker
- Be available to unblock decisions exceeding the team's autonomous authority threshold

---

## Role competencies

**Strategic**
- Product and market vision in the context of collections and finance in Latin America
- Judgment to distinguish what builds competitive advantage from what is noise
- Ability to communicate complex vision clearly to technical and non-technical audiences

**Technical**
- Deep understanding of AI architectures, LLMs, and agentic systems
- Ability to design and review agent flows, prompts, and product logic
- Knowledge of FinCo's technical stack (LiveKit, ElevenLabs, WATI, Supabase, N8N)

---

## Scope of authority

| Decision | Authority |
|---|---|
| Strategic direction and roadmap | Autonomous |
| AI architecture and high-level technical decisions | Autonomous |
| External commitments and contracts | Autonomous |
| Production changes impacting clients | Gate: CEO FinCo |
| Adding new team members | Autonomous |
| Day-to-day operational decisions | Delegated to Chief of Staff |
