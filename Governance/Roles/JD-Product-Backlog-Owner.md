# Product/Backlog Owner

## Role purpose

The Product/Backlog Owner is the bridge between clients and the development team. They understand what the business needs, translate it into actionable requirements, prioritize the backlog, and ensure the engineering team works on what matters most.

They are also the official entry point for improvements and ideas coming from clients or the business. Any request for new features or enhancements passes through this role before reaching development.

---

## Responsibilities

**Backlog management**
- Keep the product backlog updated, prioritized, and ready to be worked on in Linear
- Write user stories and acceptance criteria that the technical team can execute without ambiguity
- Ensure every item has sufficient context before entering a cycle
- Coordinate with Lead Engineering on technical feasibility of items before committing to dates

**Client → product bridge**
- Be the contact point for improvement requests and new features from clients
- Filter, prioritize, and translate client feedback into actionable backlog items
- Communicate to the client the status of their requests and the roadmap relevant to their account
- Collaborate with the commercial team in translating market needs into product features

**Product intelligence**
- Maintain visibility on what the team is building in each cycle and why
- Identify recurring patterns in client feedback that warrant structural product decisions
- Participate in the weekly Team Sync and the Friday Check/Act/Plan session
- Contribute to product decisions recorded in the operational vault

---

## Role competencies

**Product**
- Ability to listen to a client problem and translate it into a technically actionable requirement
- Judgment to prioritize under pressure and with limited resources
- Familiarity with product or project management tools (Linear or similar)

**Operational**
- Clear and direct written and verbal communication
- Autonomy to move forward without explicit instruction at every step
- Ability to work with technical teams without needing to be technical

---

## Scope of authority

| Decision | Authority |
|---|---|
| Backlog prioritization and cycle order | Autonomous |
| User story acceptance criteria | Autonomous |
| Communication of request status to clients | Autonomous |
| Rejection of items not meeting spec criteria before entering a cycle | Autonomous |
| Delivery date commitments to clients | Coordinated with Head of Operations & Product |
| Inclusion of features with architecture or compliance impact | Gate: Lead Engineering + Head of Operations & Product |
| Roadmap changes with commercial implications | Gate: CEO |
