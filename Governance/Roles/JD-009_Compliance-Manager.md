# Compliance Manager

> Initial occupant: [[Elena-Torres]]. Role created from the 2026-07-01 strategic alignment, where [[Alex Carver]] expanded compliance expectations. DRI of component 9 of the [[DRI-Map]] (Compliance Engine) and of cross-cutting axis Q3-E6 ([[Roadmap-2026]]).

> **Line of action and non-overlap of functions (RACI).** The Compliance Manager **does not direct operations**: directing operations is the responsibility of the [[JD-Operations-Manager|Operations Manager]] (vacant) under the [[JD-Director-of-Operations|Director of Operations]]. Compliance is **not separate from operations**: it has **influence** and is **consultable** (role **C** in a RACI matrix), but it is **not the Responsible/Accountable (R/A)** for operations. Its own clear line of action is compliance and quality; its "stop" authority is a **veto for compliance risk**, not operational direction.

## Role purpose

Ensure that **everything FinCo executes operates within the legal framework**: a **non-negotiable ("deal breaker")** requirement for business survival given the new Dominican Criminal Code taking effect (August 3, 2026). The Compliance Manager owns the **compliance engine**: compliance understood not as a governance layer *after* the action, but as a **real-time engine, at the source of the operation**, that validates every interaction *as it happens*.

> **Embedded quality scope.** The **quality** function lives within this role. FinCo does not create a separate Quality Manager position: we understand quality as something placed **at the source** (where things are generated), in real time, not as a downstream filter. The Compliance Manager has the scope of a Quality Manager, embedded within the compliance function.

---

## Responsibilities

**Real-time compliance engine**
- Design and maintain the controls that validate every action (calls, WhatsApp, SMS, email, data handling) **as it is being issued**, not after.
- Ensure the engine "knows everything happening in every corner" and blocks or corrects any out-of-compliance action in real time (e.g., what and how Nova says something to a debtor).
- Define hard rules: e.g., do not contact an inactive debtor, do not exceed the contact limit per day/manager.

**Legal and criminal risk management**
- Identify and map the criminal risks of the operation (Criminal Code / Criminal Procedure Code effective Aug 2026, Law 172 on data handling) and translate them into preventive controls.
- Map and prioritize the top 3 highest-volume and highest-impact judicial/extrajudicial processes.
- Provide structured risk perspective to the rest of the team: for every decision, answer "how does this affect compliance?"

**Quality at the source**
- Define interaction quality rubrics (how agents speak, how they handle debtor information) and evaluate them at the origin.
- Audit recordings and interactions; turn findings into improvements in scripts, prompts, and controls.

**Judicial automation and learning**
- Lead the automation of judicial/extrajudicial processes (dispossession, etc.) in compliance.
- Maintain, together with Atlas, the **lessons learned log** (e.g., messages sent to deactivated users) so that errors are not repeated.

**Regulatory alignment**
- Keep the operation compliant with Indotel, Proconsumidor, and policy [[POL-003_Compliance-Debtor-Dignity|POL-003 (Compliance & Debtor Dignity)]].
- Facilitate the strategic compliance session and translate its agreements into operational controls.

---

## Role competencies

**Technical**
- Dominican legal framework applicable to collections: new Criminal Code / Criminal Procedure Code, Law 172 (data), Indotel and Proconsumidor regulations.
- Design of compliance controls and quality rubrics; interaction auditing.

**Operational**
- Natural and structured risk vision, ability to identify risk points before they materialize.
- Translate legal requirements into concrete, verifiable operational rules in real time.

## Scope of authority

| Decision | Authority |
|---|---|
| Define and update compliance controls and quality rubrics | Autonomous |
| **Pause/block an action, campaign, or agent due to compliance risk** | Autonomous ("stop" authority, compliance is a deal breaker) |
| Map risks and judicial processes | Autonomous |
| Changes affecting client commitments or production | Coordinated with Director of Operations · Gate: CEO FinCo |
