# Template: FinCo Weekly Executive Report

> Used by [[WF-weekly-executive-report]]. Not a vault document itself; it is the mold that Ops-Agent (Cowork) or Atlas fills every Monday with data from the previous week (Monday to Sunday, ISO week).
>
> **Why this structure:** born from the first real execution (2026-07-06, week 2026-W27), explicitly requested by Rodney: "something more than a paragraph, with good detail, tactical executive format, mid-level management, neither too operational nor too superficial." The 7 sections cover, in this order, what Rodney asked should never be missing: meetings, operational progress, development/Linear, knowledge vault, risks, and commitments.
>
> `weekly-report-render.py` (see `WF-weekly-executive-report.md`) expects **exactly** this shape to produce the branded HTML: one `#` (H1) with the title, seven `##` (H2) numbered in this order, prose, `- ` lists and/or Markdown tables within each section. Do not rename section headers without updating the renderer.
>
> **TWO Markdown files are generated per week, not one** (Rodney's decision, 2026-07-06, after seeing the first HTML generated from the long document):
> 1. **Long version** (`YYYY-WNN.md`), detailed record, mid-management level, as described below. Stays in the vault as the week's source of truth.
> 2. **One-pager** (`YYYY-WNN-onepager.md`), same 7-section structure, compressed to 1-4 lines per section (tables where helpful, like "Meetings of the week"). It is a summary, not a cut-down version, written separately, not truncated.
>
> **The branded HTML (`YYYY-WNN.html`) is always rendered from the one-pager, never from the long document.** It is what gets distributed to executives; it must fit on one screen/page. `weekly-report-render.py` receives the path to the `-onepager.md` as argument.

## Structure

```markdown
# FinCo Weekly Review: [readable date range]

## 1. Executive Summary

[2-4 sentences. The week's headline: what was decided, what advanced, what is
the main risk or opportunity. This is the only thing someone with only
30 seconds should read.]

## 2. Meetings of the Week

[One block per day with meetings, bold the day + meeting name, then
1-3 bullets with the actual decisions/milestones, not the agenda. Source: minutes in
`Meetings/` for the week + session memory if applicable (sensitive meetings
without minutes, such as direct reviews with Alex Carver).]

## 3. Operational and Strategic Progress

[DRI Map status (closed components, in progress, lagging with their %),
Roadmap/epic changes if any, team/role structure changes.
Source: `Strategy/DRI-Roadmap-2026/DRI-Map.md`, `Roadmap-2026.md`,
`Strategy/Decisions/`.]

## 4. Development and Product: Cycle WNN (Linear)

[Cycle planning (Plan.md) vs. what was actually closed in Linear:
total closed, by person, by project, planned-vs-closed comparison,
relevant production incidents, any hygiene findings (e.g., an
issue closed but its description says unresolved). Source:
`Dev/Cycles/YYYY-WNN/Plan.md` + Linear API via `weekly-report-data.py`.]

## 5. Knowledge Vault

[Only if there was relevant activity that week: audits, remediation
plans, structural hardening (linters, generators, automated reviews),
new governance documents (DEC/SOP/POL/JD), new Knowledge-Base content.
If there was nothing, explicitly say "no governance changes this week",
do not omit the section.]

## 6. Open Risks and Blockers at Week Close

[Prioritized list, legal/regulatory deadlines first, then technical
blockers, then process gaps. Each with who is DRI if applicable.]

## 7. Commitments Carrying into Next Week

[What is coming due soon and who owns it. Mention the next Cycle Planning /
Team Sync if there is anything pending to continue there explicitly.]

---
*Review generated by [Cowork | Atlas] from FinCo-OS commits,
minutes in `Meetings/`, DRI Map, and Linear. Period covered: [range].*
```

```markdown
[1-2 sentences, the headline, nothing more]

## 2. Meetings of the Week
[Markdown table: Day | Meeting | Key decision/milestone, one row per meeting]

## 3. Operational and Strategic Progress
[1 short paragraph, 3-5 lines]

## 4. Development and Product: Cycle WNN (Linear)
[1 short paragraph with key numbers, closed, by person, incidents]

## 5. Knowledge Vault
[1-2 lines, or "no changes this week"]

## 6. Open Risks and Blockers
[1-line bullets each]

## 7. Commitments Carrying into Next Week
[1 short paragraph]

---
*Compressed version of [YYYY-WNN.md](YYYY-WNN.md). Generated by [Cowork | Atlas] (WF-weekly-executive-report).*
```

## Writing Rules

- Prose in paragraphs and bullets of 1-3 lines, never a single telegraphic line or a 15-line paragraph.
- Figures always with an implicit source (which file or query produced them), never a number that cannot be traced.
- If data is unavailable (e.g., Linear not connected, the cycle's `Review.md` does not exist yet), say so explicitly in the corresponding section instead of omitting the section or making up the data.
- Detail level in the long version: mid-management. Neither a one-sentence summary nor the operational line-by-line detail of every commit.
- Detail level in the one-pager: executive. Each section fits in 1-4 lines or a short table, if it does not fit, the detail is excessive, not the space insufficient.
- The one-pager is written from the same knowledge base as the long version (same research session); it is not a second research pass, it is a second synthesis pass.
